# Combining Badminton with Gym Training
Badminton is demanding on the legs, especially the quads and calves, because of lunges, jumps and quick direction changes.
Avoid a hard lower-body gym session on the day directly before an important badminton session. Upper-body gym sessions can be placed the day before badminton.
Count badminton time separately from gym volume. A 3-hour badminton session counts as a hard training day for fatigue purposes.
