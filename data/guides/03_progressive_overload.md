# Progressive Overload
To keep improving, gradually increase the training stress over time. For compound lifts (bench press, squat, row, overhead press, deadlift), add about 2.5 kg for upper-body lifts or 5 kg for lower-body lifts when all planned reps are completed with good form.
If you miss the planned reps at a new weight, keep the same weight next session instead of increasing it again.
